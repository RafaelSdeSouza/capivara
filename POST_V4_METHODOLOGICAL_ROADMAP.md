# Post-V4 methodological roadmap

V4 stops after establishing a trustworthy representation-specific hierarchy.
It preserves fixed `Ncomp` cuts for validation and compatibility, but a cut at
8, 24, or another requested count has no scientific privilege merely because it
is reproducible.

The next methodological question is an evidence-driven stopping rule defined on
the frozen hierarchy. Its evidence must be distinct from the representation,
the observed-entry Ward objective, numerical tie handling, and spatial support.
A future study should declare its estimand and stopping gates before examining
native partitions, then test at least null oversegmentation, recovery of known
spectral changes, stability under admissible missingness, sensitivity to support
boundaries, and behavior when the hierarchy contains raw inversions or
unresolved roots. It must retain the S/N >= 30 eligibility and object-level
coverage labels when using the validated MaNGA/Sandra shape profile.

No stopping rule is implemented or selected in V4. The five-galaxy bar pilot can
use fixed cuts as controlled analysis settings after the V4 freeze is reviewed;
its physical conclusions must not treat those cuts as inferred component counts.
